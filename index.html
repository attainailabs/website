Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

At Attain AI Labs, Inc., we respect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when visiting our website or utilizing our products.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, and company details when you submit a consultation request or join an early-access waitlist.

2. On-Device Consumer Privacy

For consumer applications such as MyWunderTale, our core engineering philosophy is privacy-by-design: personal information, voice recordings, and customized story parameters remain strictly on the user's local device without persistent raw cloud storage.

3. How We Use Business Information

We use contact inquiries solely to communicate regarding requested services, provide updates, or coordinate scheduled discovery calls. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers for specific operations: Google Analytics (aggregated web analytics) and Tally (inquiry forms). Each operates under their respective privacy commitments.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy statement or wish to exercise data rights, contact us directly at info@attainAILabs.com.
